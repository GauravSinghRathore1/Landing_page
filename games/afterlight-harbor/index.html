AFTERLIGHT HARBOR

Harbor drive

Take the wheel of a midnight-blue coupe. Follow the amber beacons for three courier jobs, or park and explore the waterfront on foot.

Drag left or right to steer. Pull down to brake, then hold to reverse. Keyboard: A/D to steer, W to accelerate, S to brake or reverse. Stop and press E to exit or enter the car.
